Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Coloring ("we", "our", or "us") provides a creative mobile coloring and drawing application crafted for children aged 3 to 10 and their parents or legal guardians. Protecting family privacy and maintaining a safe digital environment is our top priority.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled within Coloring. We follow strict child protection principles, minimizing data collection and ensuring compliance with applicable international standards including COPPA and GDPR-K.

1. Key Definitions & Overview

To keep this policy transparent and simple for families, the following terms describe how our software functions:

  • Child User: Refers to minors under 13 years of age who interact with Coloring's play modes.
  • Parent or Guardian: Refers to the adult managing device permissions, screen time limits, and subscription purchases.
  • Local Application Data: Information saved directly on your physical mobile device, such as artwork files, level completion status, and sound preferences.

Family First Privacy Principle

Coloring does not require account registration or social media logins. Children can draw, learn, and play without disclosing any personal identification.

2. Information We Collect and Store

We collect only the minimum operational data required to deliver core app functionality.

2.1 Locally Stored Operational Data

Coloring stores functional data directly on your device storage:

  • Completed drawings, custom doodles, and saved gallery templates.
  • Level completion status, star ratings (1-3 stars), earned badges, and daily streak counts.
  • App configurations such as background music volume, sound effects toggles, and parental screen time settings.

2.2 Store Transactions & Subscription Data

All monetary transactions for Weekly, Monthly, or Annual subscriptions are processed securely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play Store). We do not collect, store, or process payment card numbers or financial account details.

2.3 Aggregated Technical Diagnostics

We may receive non-identifiable, aggregated technical diagnostics (such as app crash logs and device OS versions) provided by store analytical tools to help fix bugs and improve performance across different devices.

3. How We Use Collected Information

Information processed by Coloring is strictly utilized to provide and enhance app functionality:

  • Powering core interactive flows: Gamification Level Mode, Random Page Mode, and Free Drawing Canvas.
  • Evaluating level completion accuracy, color usage metrics, and star rewards locally on the device.
  • Enforcing parental panel controls, screen time limits, and purchase gates.
  • Validating subscription status and enabling purchase restoration.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Coloring is designed specifically for children and strictly adheres to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation for Kids (GDPR-K), and mobile app store child safety guidelines.

  • No Targeted Ads: Coloring does not serve behavioral advertisements or track child users across third-party websites or services.
  • Parental Gate Protection: Access to subscription screens, external support links, and settings requires solving an adult math verification puzzle.
  • No Social Features: The app contains no chat rooms, open community forums, or public profile sharing options.

Child Protection Mandates

We do not knowingly request, collect, or transmit personal details (such as full names, email addresses, phone numbers, or real-time location) from children under 13.

5. Data Storage, Security & Offline Operations

We employ industry-standard local data security practices to protect your family's drawings and settings.

  • Offline Play Support: Core coloring templates and drawing tools function completely offline without transmitting creative content to external servers.
  • Local File Isolation: Saved artwork and progress files remain within Coloring's sandboxed local application memory.
  • No Automatic Cloud Uploads: Your child's drawings are not uploaded to public cloud servers without parent initiation.

6. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or lease any user data to third parties.

  • App Store Infrastructure: Apple App Store and Google Play Store handle in-app purchase authentication and subscription state management.
  • Diagnostic Services: Anonymized system crash reports may be processed by official platform developer tools solely to maintain application stability.

7. Data Retention & Control Rights

Parents retain full management over all local data created within Coloring on their family devices.

  • Clearing Local Gallery: Saved drawings and level progress can be removed at any time by clearing app data or uninstalling Coloring from the device.
  • Subscription Management: Subscriptions can be reviewed, modified, or canceled at any time through the parent's Apple ID or Google Play account settings.

8. Policy Updates & Contact Information

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect software additions or regulatory changes. Any updates will be published on this page with a updated effective date.

If parents or legal guardians have questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices or child protection standards, please contact our support team.

  • Review policy revisions regularly on our official store pages.
  • Direct privacy inquiries to our dedicated parental support team.
