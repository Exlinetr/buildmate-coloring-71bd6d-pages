Frequently Asked Questions

What age group is Coloring designed for?
Coloring is specifically designed for children aged 3 to 10. It features age-appropriate coloring templates, simple touch controls, adjustable brush sizes, and progressive difficulty tiers that grow with your child's skills.
How do the three play modes work?
Coloring offers three distinct play experiences: Level Mode (a gamified map with star ratings, badges, and level unlocks), Random Page Mode (stress-free casual coloring with auto-selected pages), and Free Drawing Canvas (blank canvas with textures, pencils, brushes, and step-by-step guidelines).
Can my child play Coloring without an internet connection?
Yes! Coloring fully supports offline play. Essential coloring templates, drawing tools, and local gallery functions work seamlessly without an active internet connection.
How do subscriptions work, and what content is included?
Coloring offers Weekly, Monthly, and Annual subscription options. Subscribing unlocks premium brushes (glitter, spray, texture effects), extended template libraries across all categories, and advanced gamification levels.
What parental controls are built into Coloring?
Coloring includes a dedicated Parental Panel protected by an adult authentication gate. Parents can manage screen time limits, monitor child progress reports, control in-app purchases, and adjust audio settings.
How do I restore my subscription on a new device?
Open the app, enter the Parental Panel by solving the math gate, tap on Account Settings, and select 'Restore Purchases'. Ensure you are signed into the same App Store or Google Play account used for original purchase.

Troubleshooting

App freezes or closes unexpectedly
Ensure your device operating system is updated to the latest version. Close background applications and restart Coloring. If the issue persists, verify that your device has sufficient free storage space.
Purchased subscription features remain locked
Access the Parental Panel, open Settings, and tap 'Restore Purchases'. Confirm that your device is logged into the Apple ID or Google Play account used during purchase.
Sound effects or background music are silent
Check that device main volume is turned up and device hardware silent switch is turned off. Open Coloring settings in the Parental Panel to verify background music and sound toggles are enabled.
Level progression is locked
Levels unlock sequentially upon achieving at least 1 star on the preceding level. For levels beyond free preview tiers, verify that your account has an active subscription.
Artwork fails to save to the gallery
Verify that Coloring has permission to access local device storage. Check your device storage capacity to ensure adequate space for high-resolution images.
